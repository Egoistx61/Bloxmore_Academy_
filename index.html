Lineages & Heritage

Choose Your RACE

Upon awakening, your race dictates innate passives, agility thresholds, and synergy with specific combat abilities.

[ RACE: TELEKINESIS ]

Telekinesis

Mythic

A supernatural race that allows players to manipulate objects using the power of their mind. Telekinetics can move, throw, and control objects from a distance, turning the environment itself into a weapon.

Innate Trait: Mind Over Matter
Passive Buff: +25% Psionic | Shield
[ RACE: HYDE ]

Hyde

Uncommon

One of the rarest and most powerful races in Bloxmore. Hyde allows the player to transform into a terrifying creature with enhanced strength and devastating attacks. Its transformation completely changes the player's appearance and combat style.

Innate Trait: Primal Surge
Passive Buff: +30% ATK | +20% Tenacity
[ RACE: WEREWOLF ]

Werewolf

Rare

A race with the ability to transform into a powerful wolf form. While transformed, the player gains access to a unique set of attacks, including powerful M1 claw strikes and enhanced physical abilities.

Innate Trait: Lunar Frenzy
Passive Buff: +20% Speed | Bleed Hit
[ RACE: GORGONS ]

Gorgons

Ancient

A mysterious race with the ability to turn opponents to stone. Gorgons can use their supernatural gaze to temporarily petrify enemies, giving them a powerful advantage in combat.

Innate Trait: Petrifying Gaze
Passive Buff: Stun Chance | +15% Acid
[ RACE: HUMAN ]

Humans

Adaptive

The standard starter race for every player. Humans have no supernatural abilities or transformations, but they provide a simple starting point for players to explore Bloxmore and discover what lies beyond the ordinary.

Innate Trait: Unyielding Spirit
Passive Buff: +25% EXP | Versatile
[ RACE: SONORIS ]

Sonaris

Rare

A rare race that harnesses sound as a weapon. Sonaris users can produce extremely loud supernatural sounds and use powerful sonic attacks to damage and disrupt opponents.

Innate Trait: Resonant Frequency
Passive Buff: +25% Sonic AoE | Disruption Aura
